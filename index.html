A NEW THREAT

It is a period of digital war. Fraudulent invoices move through the accounts payable systems of a thousand worlds, draining treasuries before anyone notices the signature is wrong.

From a seed-stage outpost called APTOSI, a founding engineer builds the countermeasures — static analysis that halts a corrupted pull request before it merges, authenticated scans that surface injection flaws before an adversary does.

Meanwhile, on the training worlds of SENECA POLYTECHNIC, he writes firmware that must answer in microseconds, and teaches neural networks to see what human reviewers miss.

Pursued by deadlines and compliance labs alike, he continues his work — because the perimeter only holds if someone keeps testing it...
